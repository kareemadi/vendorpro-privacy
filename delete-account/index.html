Delete your VendorPro account

VendorPro, by Kareem Adi · Last updated: November 25, 2026

You can delete your VendorPro account and all of its data at any time, from inside the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open VendorPro and sign in.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away, and this can't be undone.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email support@getvendorpro.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my VendorPro account." If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, tell us that and we'll help you confirm the account is yours. We'll delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, and sign-in details
  • Your inventory, purchases, sales, trades, and show sessions
  • Card photos you saved
  • Price alerts and your device's push notification token

What is kept

  • Card show listings you added to the app's public show directory stay listed for other vendors, but they are no longer connected to you or your account.

Delete your data but keep your account

To clear your inventory and records without closing your account, go to settings and tap Delete everything. Your account stays open and empty.
