FAQ

Questions vendors ask.

Who is VendorPro for?

People who sell cards at shows, from a first table to every weekend. If you buy, sell and trade in person and want to know what you actually made, it's for you.

Which cards does it support?

Pokémon (English and Japanese), One Piece and sports cards, plus graded slabs and sealed product like booster boxes and cases.

Where do the prices come from?

TCGplayer market prices for raw cards and sealed product, plus a one-tap link to eBay sold listings for any card, slabs included. Your inventory is repriced every night.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Sales and buys are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a CSV, and export your inventory and history whenever you want.

Is there an Android version?

It's on the way. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once when it's on Google Play.

What happens to my data?

It's yours. Read the privacy policy for details, or email support@getvendorpro.app.
