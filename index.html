VendorPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

VendorPro is an inventory and profit-tracking app for trading card vendors. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
  • Business data you enter. Inventory items, purchase and sale records, trade records, show sessions, prices, notes, and locations you type into the app.
  • Photos. Photos of cards that you take with the camera or choose from your photo library. The app only accesses photos you explicitly select or capture.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the app: to show your inventory, calculate your profit and cash flow, and identify and price your cards. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Card scanning and price lookups

  • Scanning. When you scan a card, the photo is processed on your device (text recognition) and may also be sent, through our server, to Anthropic's Claude API to identify the card. The image is used only to return the card's name, set, and number; we do not keep it unless you choose to save it as the card's photo.
  • Prices. To look up market prices, the app sends card names and set or number identifiers (not personal information) to public card databases: TCGdex and optcgapi.com.

Where your data is stored

Your account, inventory, and transaction data are stored with Supabase (our database, authentication, and file-storage provider). Card photos you save are stored in a file bucket; each photo is reachable by its direct link, so anyone who has that exact link could view it. Links are not listed or published by the app.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything under it, including inventory, transactions, sessions, and photos, from inside the app: tap the settings icon (top right), then Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. You can also clear your business data while keeping your account using Delete everything on the same screen.

Shared show listings you created may remain in the app's public show directory, but are detached from your account.

Children

VendorPro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
