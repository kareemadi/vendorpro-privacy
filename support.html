Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app, tap the settings icon (top right), scroll to Account, and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all of your data.

A card scan picked the wrong card. What do I do?
Scanning shows a list of likely matches. Pick the right one, or dismiss the list and search by name or by the number printed on the card (for example 022/084).

Why does a card show N/A for market price?
Prices come from TCGplayer. If TCGplayer has no listing for that exact printing, the app shows N/A rather than guessing.
